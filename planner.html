Before connecting your accounts

Please read these risks before saving keys or using online features.

  • Keys are saved unencrypted in this browser. Password fields hide their display, not their storage. Anyone with access to your browser profile, sufficiently privileged extensions, or malicious code on the same origin may access them.
  • Requests send credentials to their intended services. Instagram receives your token; your chosen webhook receives its key. The optional local relay handles the webhook key in memory.
  • Network captures and errors may reveal secrets. Instagram tokens appear in request URLs, and upstream errors may echo sensitive information. Redact screenshots, logs and HAR files before sharing.
  • Uploaded photos have accessible URLs. Cloudinary receives JPEG copies. Protect your unsigned upload preset, restrict it in Cloudinary, monitor usage and manage deletion there.
  • Use a trusted local copy. Do not enter keys in an unfamiliar hosted or modified copy. Backups contain photos and plans. Clear saved keys does not revoke provider credentials.
  • Publishing needs your approval. The receiver controls publishing; configure it to ask you first. This notice is an acknowledgement, not a security guarantee.
